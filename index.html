The guide

Built by someone
who did it anyway.

Noros Solutions was created by a PhD mechanical engineer, AP Computer Science teacher, certified doula, and mom of two based in New Jersey. A decade of teaching at the university and high school level. Real tools built and deployed for real people. An LLC started between early morning sessions before the rest of the house woke up.

The throughline across everything — teaching, engineering, AI, education, consulting — is this: the most powerful guide is someone who has walked the exact path you're on. Not someone who studied it. Someone who lived it.

That's what Noros is built on.

The name
Noros is a combination of neuro — the science of learning and intelligence — and oros, the Greek word for mountain. It's not just a name. It's a philosophy: that the climb toward understanding is the point, and that the right guide makes all the difference.
