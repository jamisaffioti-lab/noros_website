How it works

Simple, focused,
no surprises.

1
You apply
Fill out the consultation request below. Tell Jami about your business, your biggest pain point, and what you've already tried. She reads every application personally within 24 hours.
2
Discovery call
If it looks like a fit, Jami schedules a 30-minute discovery call. No pitch, no hard sell — just a real conversation about your situation and whether Summit Advisory can genuinely help. If it's not the right fit, she'll tell you that too.
3
Proposal and agreement
A clear scope, a fixed price, and a timeline. No billable-hour surprises. Payment terms: 50% deposit to begin, 50% on delivery. You own everything that's built — no recurring license fees, no vendor lock-in.
4
Build and deploy
Jami builds, tests, and deploys your solution. You're involved at key checkpoints — not buried in daily status updates. When it's done, it works. And Jami walks you through how to maintain it yourself.
5
Handoff and independence
You get the code, the documentation, and a walkthrough. The goal is your independence, not your dependency. If something breaks six months later, Jami is reachable — but most clients don't need her after handoff.
