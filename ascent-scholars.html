Meet Beta

Not a chatbot.
A companion.

Most AI tools give students access to everything — which means they get answers to questions they haven't asked yet, from sources they haven't studied, on topics that aren't on their exam.

Beta is different. You build it yourself. You choose your guide. You name your companion. You add your exact courses and curriculum sources. And Beta only pulls from those.

It's not just a study tool — it's a reflection of how you learn. The guide you choose shapes how it talks to you. The courses you add define what it knows. The name you pick makes it yours.

The core mechanic
