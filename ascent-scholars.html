Meet Beta

Not a chatbot.
A companion.

Most AI tools give students access to everything — which means they get answers to questions they haven't asked yet, from sources they haven't studied, on topics that aren't on their exam.

Beta is different. You build it yourself. You choose your guide. You name your companion. You add your exact courses and curriculum sources. And Beta only pulls from those.

It's not just a study tool — it's a reflection of how you learn. The guide you choose shapes how it talks to you. The courses you add define what it knows. The name you pick makes it yours.

The core mechanic
"Always in beta. Always getting better — just like you."

The AI Architect

Most people use AI.
Architects understand it.

A founding cohort of ~10 students. Real math, real code, real understanding.

1
Language and probability
Bigram matrices, tokenization, and how language models predict the next word from mathematical patterns.
2
Neural networks from scratch
Perceptrons, activation functions, backpropagation — built line by line in Python, not imported from a library.
3
Training and learning
Gradient descent, loss functions, and why AI "learns" — explained through the math, not the metaphor.
4
Transformers and attention
The architecture behind GPT, Claude, and every modern language model — demystified for a high school mind.
5
Build your own model
The capstone: students build and train a small language model on a dataset of their choosing. Yours to keep.
